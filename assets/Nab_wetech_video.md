### **4. WHY SDLC MATTERS (1 minute)**
<!-- **Remember: Quality Gates Story → My Example** -->

SDLC's **quality gates** that save projects.

Think of it like this:
- **Requirements** → Build the RIGHT thing
- **Design** → Catch mistakes BEFORE coding
- **Testing** → Find bugs BEFORE users do
- **Deployment** → Launch reliably
- **Maintenance** → Keep it healthy

Real example: My HCMUT thesis project - the book rental system.

I followed SDLC: wrote requirements, designed database, built incrementally, tested thoroughly.

Result? 10,000 visits. Zero critical bugs. Score 9.5 out of 10.

SDLC turns ideas into reliable software. Skip it? You're building on sand.

Let me tell you what happens when teams skip SDLC.
